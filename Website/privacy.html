Privacy

Updated October 23, 2026. This website is operated by Jon Rogol.

Optional visitor analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics,” this site uses Google Analytics 4 to estimate how many people visit, which pages they view, and how they arrive. Google receives information such as page addresses, referral sources, browser and device information, approximate location, and interactions such as page views. Query strings and fragments are removed from page and referral addresses sent by this site.

Analytics cookies, including _ga and a property-specific _ga_… cookie, help recognize returning browsers. They expire after up to six months, and may be refreshed when you return. Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled. This site does not send names, email addresses, or contact-form entries to Analytics.

Google Analytics is loaded only after you allow it. Declining keeps it off. Your choice is stored in your browser for up to 180 days; if browser storage is unavailable, it applies only to the current page. You can change your choice below or through “Analytics settings” in the footer. Declining after previously allowing Analytics disables further measurement and removes this site’s Analytics cookies. Previously collected data is not erased by changing this setting.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services, Google’s privacy policy, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Website delivery

Amazon Web Services hosts and delivers this site through S3 and CloudFront. Your browser sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address and requested files, to deliver the pages. This is separate from the optional Analytics choice.
