03 / How I work

The whole path matters.

I work with network teams, project teams, carriers, and vendors to prepare changes, resolve problems, and verify the result—from a new site’s first connection to recovery after an outage.

  1. 01

    Trace the full path

    Follow the dependencies across routing, security, delivery, and core services to find the real source of a problem.

  2. 02

    Make changes carefully

    Prepare the change, coordinate the cutover, and validate service afterward. During incidents, keep teams and leadership informed as the investigation progresses.

  3. 03

    Leave teams with answers

    Document what failed and how it was resolved. Maintain useful diagrams and runbooks, and help other analysts strengthen their troubleshooting.
